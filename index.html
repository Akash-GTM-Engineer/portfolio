How I build

From a raw list to a ready record.

  1. Define who to sell toICP, personas and account tiers from firmographics and tech stack.
  2. Source and enrichApollo, Sales Navigator and scraping, then Clay waterfalls for emails and phones.
  3. Find the reason to reach outBuying signals like ERP or AI tool usage, hiring and news decide priority and the hook.
  4. Sequence, sync, reportMulti-touch LinkedIn and email, replies routed to the CRM, results in Looker Studio.
